## 🧩 Website Sections

### 🌤️ Hero Section

Introduces the topic with a strong headline, animated statistics, and call-to-action buttons, followed by a scrolling ticker of common problems.

### 😞 Main Struggles

Eight major problems patients face:

* Excessive visit fees
* Long waiting times
* Unnecessary tests
* Expensive branded medicines
* Overcrowded hospitals
* Lack of doctors in villages
* No medical record keeping
* Disrespectful treatment of patients

### 💰 Cost Analysis

A detailed breakdown of what a single doctor visit costs, including visit fees, tests, medicines and travel, along with statistics on loans, asset sales and treatment dropouts.

### 🛠️ Remedies

Practical solutions split into two groups:

* **Personal:** government hospitals, community clinics, telemedicine, health insurance, generic medicines and medical records
* **Collective:** fee regulation, more doctors, digital health system, higher health budget, accountability and rural health infrastructure

### 🥗 Prevention

Lifestyle tips to reduce the need for doctor visits:

* Balanced diet
* Regular exercise
* Enough sleep
* Timely vaccination
* Quitting smoking and alcohol
* Mental health care
* Regular checkups

### 📞 Emergency Contacts

Free healthcare and emergency hotline numbers for quick access.

### ❓ FAQ

Common questions answered in an interactive accordion, such as free treatment in government hospitals, unnecessary tests, generic medicines, telemedicine and financial aid.

### 📝 Share Your Experience

A form where visitors can share their own experience of the struggles of visiting a doctor.
